[ ] Battery % — remaining issues (diagnosed 2026-07-06, ported to CYD-Crypto-Tracker)
    - EMA alpha 0.12 too low → filter slowly chases load sag, % ticks down every refresh
    - BATT_SAG_OFFSET_MV 30mV too small → real WiFi+display sag is 100-300mV at battery
    - No display hysteresis → every tiny EMA change redraws %, looks jittery
    - Sharp piecewise curve boundaries → ADC noise crossing 0.1V segment = % jumps
    - Samples collide with WiFi TX bursts → sporadic low readings
    Proposed Layer 1: alpha→0.25, sag→100mV at pin, ±2% display hysteresis
    Proposed Layer 2: smooth polynomial LiPo curve instead of piecewise
    Proposed Layer 3: median filter or WiFi-idle-gated sampling
    See CYD-Crypto-Tracker/TODO.md for full diagnosis.
